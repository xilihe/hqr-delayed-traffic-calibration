Confirmation design — working, not frozen

The source classifier and historical maps are fitted before October 2024. All flow values on or after 1 October remain inaccessible until a sealed protocol and implementation manifest are written. July–September results are development evidence; the confirmation period is October 2024–May 2025, using every available date and up to 6,000 uniformly sampled flows per date. Missing dates are retained as calendar gaps, not filled with synthetic observations. The audit queue and 28-calendar-day history continue from the complete July–September warmup.

The main candidate is historical predicted-class 95th-quantile recentering (HQR) followed by one pooled rolling quantile. It is an application of additive conditional-quantile score rectification, not a claim to have invented general score rectification. The 95% level, 20-row mapping prior, disjoint historical roles, 28-day window, strict rejection rule and target alpha=0.05 are fixed from development. The main score is negative energy. Maximum-softmax complement and source-only Mahalanobis distance are score sensitivity analyses. There is no model refitting on audit labels.

Source model seeds are fixed to 42, 43 and 44. The source rows, class partition, source preprocessing and initial calibration sampling remain identical across model seeds; source and calibration sampling seeds are 42 and 43. Audit seeds are 101, 102 and 103. Seeds are computational replications on the same traffic, not independent networks or participants. Additional model fits use exactly the reused Transformer configuration and 50 source epochs. The seed42 weights are reused. Known-service selection remains the single source-defined partition, and conclusions refer to that partition and collection setting.

Daily query budgets are 32, 128 and 512, with one- and seven-calendar-day delays. Every requested flow, including unknown traffic, consumes budget. Common audit requests are used across all methods. Comparators are fixed global, fixed predicted-class, rolling global, rolling predicted-class, threshold shrinkage and delayed per-revealed-known-label ACI. Multi-anchor historical reference-quantile alignment is a mechanism control with the same mapping-fit subset, prior and disjoint initial risk-calibration subset. Development showed no additional gain from its seven anchors in the primary energy/32-label/seven-day setting; retain it as a comparison rather than expanding its tuning. Only rolling-global calibration is needed for these two historical transformations; the five threshold rules and selected delayed ACI are compared on the raw score. For each score, budget and delay, ACI step size is chosen from the declared development grid by minimizing mean daily absolute deviation of known false rejection from 0.05, averaged over the three audit seeds. This equals twice mean positive excess minus (mean false rejection minus 0.05). Ties favor the smaller step size. Raw and RQP development ACI use the same criterion; the frozen main comparison uses raw-score ACI, with the normalized development grid retained in the audit trail. Winning values and all development grid values must be recorded before sealing. No confirmation result can revise this choice.

The primary setting is energy score, 32 labels/day and seven-day delay. Report known false rejection and correct-and-accepted rate jointly with held-out-service and background-group detection. The two unknown populations remain separate. Development period aggregation located the effect in background-group rejection; held-out-service macro detection was essentially unchanged. The main improvement hypothesis therefore concerns background rejection at comparable known false rejection, with held-out-service detection reported as a transfer boundary. This choice is made before any confirmation flow values are opened. Period macro detection first sums each true class's counts over the confirmation period and then weights represented classes equally. Mean daily false rejection weights observed dates equally. Mean daily true-class macro results are secondary estimands. A day marginally above 5% is not evidence of violation of a per-day guarantee; no such arbitrary-drift guarantee is asserted.

Primary paired contrasts compare HQR rolling-global calibration with raw rolling-global calibration and multi-anchor historical reference alignment. Detection gains must be interpreted alongside achieved false rejection. A 0.5-percentage-point tolerance in mean daily known false rejection is the pre-confirmation operational noninferiority margin for descriptive interpretation; this is an authoring decision, not a theorem or an externally validated service-level agreement. All measured rates and paired differences are reported even when that criterion is not met. Comparisons with other calibrated methods and the two additional budgets/delays show the operating trade-off.

Uncertainty uses paired moving calendar blocks of 28 days. Resampled blocks use the same dates for every comparator, model and audit seed. Each bootstrap sample recomputes class-aggregate ratios, then averages the nine model/audit cells. Missing calendar dates have no observations. Use 2,000 bootstrap replicates and fixed resampling seed 20261122. Intervals describe temporal variation for this fixed collection and fixed set of model/audit replications; model-seed ranges are also reported separately. The primary effect estimates are percentage-point differences and 95% intervals, without a binary significance claim across the full grid. Seven- and 56-day blocks are sensitivity analyses for primary contrasts. No independence assumption is made for adjacent flow rows or for repeated audit seeds.

The canonical-input-disjoint sensitivity retains the same thresholds and query histories and excludes evaluation inputs equal to source-fitting or historical-calibration inputs. Identical packet prefixes do not establish duplicate flows. Report the excluded fraction and interpret this as input-overlap sensitivity.

Stopping rule: evaluate the frozen confirmation once, retain negative or inconclusive results, complete independent score-transform and decision/count audits, and write claims to match the confirmed operating trade-off. Repair an implementation error only after preserving the original evidence and recording which findings are invalidated; never relabel examined confirmation data as untouched.
